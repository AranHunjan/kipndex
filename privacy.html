Privacy policy

KipNDex Publisher

Effective 6 November 2026

Who uses it

KipNDex Publisher is a private tool run by the owner of the Kip N Dex channel. Only the channel owner signs in to it, and it only works with the Kip N Dex accounts.

What it can access

When the channel owner signs in, they give the tool permission to:

  • YouTube: upload Kip N Dex videos, add caption tracks, and post comments on the Kip N Dex channel.
  • Facebook and Instagram: publish Reels and comments on the Kip N Dex Facebook Page and Instagram account.
  • TikTok: upload Kip N Dex videos to the Kip N Dex account.

What it stores

The sign-in tokens, and a log of what was posted (post links and IDs). Both are kept only on the channel owner's computer. The tool never sees or stores passwords.

What it doesn't do

It doesn't collect, sell or share anyone's personal data. It doesn't read other people's accounts, show ads, or use cookies or analytics.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visits for security. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Google user data

KipNDex Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The channel owner can remove the tool's access at any time:

  • Google: Google Account > Security > Your connections to third-party apps and services.
  • Facebook and Instagram: Facebook Settings > Business integrations.
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions.

The saved tokens are then deleted from the computer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Reach us through the Kip N Dex YouTube channel.
